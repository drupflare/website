Gregory started writing code with Drupal and PHP in late 2018, building websites inside brightplum,
his father's shop. Drupflare is that first framework coming back around: the goal is to run the
Drupal sites people already have, unchanged, without a server underneath them.

Before that came Scratch projects at eight, Discord bots, a C++ terminal game and years of Minecraft
plugins. He has sent pull requests to projects he does not own, answered bug reports from strangers
on his own, and spent two summers teaching Computer Science to 7th through 9th graders in Chicago.

He also built The Earth App, a live mobile app aimed at loneliness, and is now at Dartmouth majoring
in Computer Science with a minor in Quantitative Social Science.
